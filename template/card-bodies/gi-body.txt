IDEA GATE — lane <N>. A person holds this card: no worker runs it, and the driver never commits. Nothing is committed unless you choose to commit it.

Why it exists: the plan, tests and code are all built on the idea you accept here, and an idea is far cheaper to fix now than a plan later.

WHAT YOU DO (from this card, in the dashboard or the CLI):
1. Wait for the driver's comment starting "GATE READY" on this card. Until it appears the gate is still waiting for its input, and a verdict you write is not applied (the driver answers "NOT APPLIED" and says why).
2. Read <REFINED> against the raw idea at <IDEA>: Open questions first, then Success criteria and the Verification recipe. A line marked `manual at Gc` is one you check yourself at the code gate.
3. Answer with a COMMENT on this card whose first word is the verdict:
   PASS   (or PASS: <your words>) — accept; the plan card starts.
   REWORK: <what is wrong, with your answers> — the researcher revises and you get a re-gate card (up to the board's rework cap, then the lane escalates). The reason is required: the revision is built from it.
   The driver applies it within a minute and completes this card with your words.
4. Optional, before PASS: edit <REFINED> yourself (you are the idea's author; the researcher only sharpened it), or inspect and commit what is staged (git diff --cached --stat). Committing is never required.

DO NOT — each of these stops the board or skips your decision:
- block this card: a gate blocked by hand can halt the board;
- move it to another column (Done included) or archive it: use the comment, which records your verdict;
- put the verdict anywhere but the first word of a comment: other comments are ignored.

With auto-gates on, the driver completes this card itself on the evidence and you do nothing.

CLI alternative: hermes kanban --board <BOARD> complete <YOUR-CARD-ID> --result "PASS: accepted"   (or --result "REWORK: <why>")
Do not open the gate on a refinement you do not believe: fix it first, or send it back with REWORK.
